ABOUT CPSERC

Prepared to Serve.
Ready to Respond.

CPSERC is a public safety and response organization focused on providing assistance during emergencies, incidents, activities, and situations where trained responders are needed.

Trained Members

Members are organized and prepared to assist during emergency and public safety situations.

Emergency Response

Our members stand ready to provide immediate assistance whenever their presence is needed.

Community Service

We work together to promote safety, preparedness, cooperation, and community support.

OUR MISSION

Always Ready.
Always Prepared.

To establish a reliable and organized public safety response corps that is prepared to assist individuals and communities during emergencies, events, disasters, and other situations requiring immediate assistance.
