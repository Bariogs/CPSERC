WHAT THE NAME STANDS FOR

Breaking down CPSERC

  • C
    ColegioThe college the corps is organized under — Colegio de San Francisco Javier.
  • P
    PublicIts work reaches beyond the classroom, into the barangay and municipality it serves.
  • S
    SafetyEveryday readiness — first aid coverage, patrols, and safety checks at school events.
  • E R
    Emergency ResponseTrained assistance during disasters, accidents, and other emergencies affecting the community.
  • C
    CorpAn organized student unit with officers, duty rosters, and attendance discipline — which is what this system tracks.

ABOUT THIS SITE

What this website does

This is CPSERC's digital attendance logbook. Officers set a duty schedule and review every check-in; members log in to record their attendance, with a photo and location captured at the moment of check-in.
